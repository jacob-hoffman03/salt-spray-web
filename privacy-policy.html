Privacy Policy

Salt & Spray (“Salt & Spray,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you visit saltandspray.com, contact us, request an estimate, submit an advertising lead form, or otherwise interact with our painting and specialty-finishing services.

Information We Collect

We may collect information that you provide directly to us, including:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Telephone number
  • Project address or city
  • Information about your property or requested services
  • Project descriptions, photographs, preferred timing, and appointment information
  • Communications you send through forms, email, telephone, text message, social media, or advertising platforms

Our website and third-party service providers may also automatically collect limited technical information, including your IP address, browser type, device information, referring website, pages viewed, and information collected through cookies or similar technologies.

How We Collect Information

We may receive information through:

  • Our website and contact forms
  • Facebook, Instagram, Google, and other advertising or social-media platforms
  • Telephone calls, text messages, and emails
  • Estimate requests and project consultations
  • Cookies, analytics tools, and advertising technologies
  • Referrals or communications from homeowners, property representatives, designers, contractors, or other business partners

How We Use Information

We may use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to inquiries and estimate requests
  • Contact you about your painting or specialty-finishing project
  • Schedule consultations, estimates, and services
  • Prepare proposals and communicate about project details
  • Provide and improve our services and customer experience
  • Maintain business records
  • Understand the effectiveness of our website and advertising
  • Protect our website, customers, team, and business
  • Comply with applicable laws and legal obligations

If you provide a telephone number, we may call or text you regarding your inquiry or project. Message and data rates may apply. You may reply STOP to opt out of text messages.

How We Disclose Information

We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to:

  • Employees, contractors, or team members helping respond to your inquiry or perform your project
  • Website hosting, communications, scheduling, analytics, advertising, customer-management, and technology providers
  • Professional advisers such as accountants, attorneys, or insurers
  • Government authorities or other parties when required by law or necessary to protect legal rights, safety, or property
  • A successor or acquiring organization in connection with a business transaction

We do not sell personal information for monetary consideration. We do not sell telephone numbers or text-message consent information. Mobile information will not be shared with third parties for their own marketing purposes. Information may be provided to vendors that support our communications and business operations.

Cookies, Analytics, And Advertising

Our website or third-party platforms may use cookies and similar technologies to operate the website, understand visitor activity, measure advertising effectiveness, and improve our services.

Third parties such as Google and Meta may process information according to their own privacy policies. You may control cookies through your browser settings, although disabling cookies may affect certain website functions.

Our website does not currently respond to browser “Do Not Track” signals. Third-party providers may collect information over time and across websites according to their own policies and technologies.

How Long We Retain Information

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to inquiries, provide services, maintain appropriate business and tax records, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and meet legal obligations.

Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures intended to protect personal information. However, no website, electronic transmission, or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your Choices And Requests

You may ask us to review, correct, or delete personal information we maintain about you, subject to applicable legal and recordkeeping requirements.

You may also opt out of promotional communications at any time by following the unsubscribe instructions in an email, replying STOP to a text message, or contacting us directly.

To make a privacy request, email tristen@saltandspray.com or call 949-593-9085. We may need to verify your identity before completing certain requests.

Children’s Privacy

Our website and services are intended for homeowners, property representatives, and other adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to websites or services operated by other organizations. Salt & Spray is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those third-party services.

Changes To This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our services, technologies, or legal obligations. Updated versions will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
