    12 Stage Process
    1. 01
      Booking & Contact

      Contact us by phone, text, email, or through the website to share your goals, timeline, and project details.

      Estimated Time: 5-15 minutes
    2. 02
      Discovery Call

      A brief consultation to learn more, explain the process, and decide whether an on-site walkthrough is the right next step.

      Estimated Time: 10-20 minutes
    3. 03
      Project Walkthrough

      We inspect included areas, existing conditions, repairs, product options, and finishes so the scope is accurate.

      Standard Duration: 45-90 minutes
    4. 04
      Proposal Review

      We review scope, materials, systems, options, warranties, maintenance plans, timeline, and remaining questions.

      Standard Duration: 30-60 minutes
    5. 05
      Project Approval

      You approve the proposal and agreement, submit the required deposit, and we prepare the project for production.

    6. 06
      Project Scheduling

      We coordinate scheduling, materials, crew availability, and any special access requirements.

    7. 07
      Pre-Construction Consultation

      We review final colors, product selections, access, furniture protection, pets, landscaping, parking, and special requests.

      Typical Duration: 15-30 minutes
    8. 08
      24-Hour Project Confirmation

      One business day before the scheduled start, we confirm arrival time, project readiness, and any last-minute questions.

    9. 09
      Project Production

      Our team protects the home, completes prep, repairs, restoration, masking, and finish applications while providing updates.

    10. 10
      Internal Quality Inspection

      Before presenting the project, our team verifies the work meets Salt & Spray standards and completes touch-ups.

    11. 11
      Final Homeowner Walkthrough

      We walk the completed areas together, review the work, answer questions, and address final touch-ups if needed.

      Typical Duration: 15-30 minutes
    12. 12
      Project Closeout & Ongoing Support

      We provide the final invoice, payment receipt, answer remaining questions, and review maintenance recommendations.

    About Us

    Homeowners deserve more than a contractor. They deserve a trusted partner who treats their home with professionalism, respect, and genuine pride.

    Craftsmanship Accountability Communication Respect

    Salt & Spray Painting & Faux Finishes was built on the belief that homeowners deserve more than a contractor. They deserve a trusted partner who treats their home with professionalism, respect, and genuine pride in the finished product.

    We are a locally owned company based in Dana Point, proudly serving Coastal Orange County. What began as a vision, a commitment to craftsmanship, and countless hours spent earning the trust of one homeowner at a time has grown into a company built almost entirely through referrals, relationships, and a reputation for doing things the right way.

    Wood staining and restoration are a central part of our craft. We restore entry doors, trim, railings, decks, cabinetry, and other architectural wood by addressing the existing finish first, carefully preparing the surface, matching or developing the stain color, and selecting a protective finish suited to the wood and its exposure.

    This company was not created simply to paint homes. It was created to help restore something that has slowly disappeared from much of the construction industry: pride in craftsmanship, accountability, exceptional customer service, and the commitment to doing the job right even when no one is watching.

    Too many homeowners have experienced contractors who rush through estimates, communicate poorly, leave projects unfinished, or cut corners to save time. We believe that should never be considered normal.

    From the moment you contact Salt & Spray, our goal is to provide an experience that feels different. Our process is intentional. We begin by understanding your goals, your home, and your vision before creating a detailed proposal tailored specifically to your project.

    We take the time to educate you on products, preparation methods, finishes, and long-term maintenance so you can make informed decisions with confidence. Once your project begins, you receive clear communication, dedicated project management, and a team that values professionalism as much as craftsmanship.

    Every brush stroke, every repaired surface, every custom stain, and every faux finish reflects our commitment to excellence. We do not believe in shortcuts because shortcuts rarely create lasting results. Instead, we believe in proper preparation, premium materials, proven application methods, and attention to the details that many companies overlook.

    Our work is about more than creating beautiful spaces. It is about protecting one of the biggest investments you will ever make, increasing the longevity of your finishes, preserving the character of your home, and delivering results you will be proud to see every day.

    As a family-owned business, we understand the importance of home. It is where birthdays are celebrated, families gather, children grow up, and memories are made. We never lose sight of the responsibility that comes with being invited into those spaces.

    Our team shares one common purpose: to leave every project better than we found it and every client more confident in their decision to hire us.

    We are not trying to be the biggest painting company in California. We are striving to become the company that sets the standard for what professionalism, craftsmanship, and customer care should look like in our industry.

    That is who we are. That is what Salt & Spray stands for.
